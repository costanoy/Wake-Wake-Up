## Full description (1688 of 4000)

```
Wake Wake Up is an alarm clock for people who sleep too deeply for snooze to work.

The alarm rings until you get up. To turn it off, you have to finish a mission: solve 1 to 10 math problems at 3 difficulty levels, or type the phrase of the day. Get it wrong and you try again. No switching it off on reflex with your eyes closed.

HOW IT WORKS
- The alarm rings, with the volume rising from low to full over 60 seconds if you want.
- Instead of a classic snooze there is Snooze: a 5-minute pause with no limit on uses. When time is up, the alarm rings again.
- To stop it for good, you start the mission and finish it.
- On the Good morning screen, the app shows how long it took you to really wake up.

ALSO INCLUDED
- App sounds, your phone's ringtones, a song from your device or an internet radio station. If the station doesn't play, the regular alarm takes over.
- Skip just one day: the alarm stays on and rings again the next day.
- Already up?: if you are already using your phone up to 1 hour before a morning alarm, a silent notification asks whether to skip it just this once. To confirm, the app asks for a quick math problem.
- 7-day stats: the time between the first ring and the finished mission, with the weekly average.
- A 1970s clock-radio look with flip digits, in English and Portuguese.

PRIVACY
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your alarms and history stay on your device. The app only uses the internet for the radio feature.

PERMISSIONS
To ring on the minute and open over the lock screen, the app asks for notifications, exact alarms and full-screen access. It explains each one the first time you open it.

Questions and suggestions: cyberhat.tech@gmail.com
```
