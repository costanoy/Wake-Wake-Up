Day off?

Skip just the next ring of a repeating alarm. It rings again on its own the time after.

Up before the alarm?

If you're already using your phone up to an hour before a morning alarm, the app asks whether to turn that alarm off just this once. To confirm, you solve a quick sum.

Stuck on a problem?

Tap I can't solve this and type the phrase of the day instead, in the time left.
